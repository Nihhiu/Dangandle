        How to play

        1. One character is hidden. You have 10 attempts to name them.
        2. Type a name and pick a suggestion (aliases like Keebo or Celeste work too).
        3. Each guess is compared with the secret on nine attributes: title, gender, height, weight, status, talent, likes, dislikes and debut game.
        4. means the attribute matches exactly, means it is partially right, means it is wrong.
        5. Numbers also point up or down: your height was too low or too high.
        6. The counter shows how many characters are still consistent with every clue you have seen.
        7. A new puzzle is generated every day from the date as a seed — everybody plays the same character on the same day.

        Progress and statistics are stored in this browser only.
